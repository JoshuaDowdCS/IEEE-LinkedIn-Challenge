## Challenge Details

- What is it?
    
    We challenge people to post on LinkedIn once a week for the month of April, starting the first Monday, Apr. 6th. You can post about anything you want! We want people to be creative and talk about things they find interesting.
    
- Why do it?
    
    It is an opportunity to improve your professional portfolio and connect with other students. We will also be giving away two $40 gift cards!
    
- How do you get the gift cards?
    
    There will be two ways to get a gift card! The person who has the most growth on LinkedIn will get one of the gift cards. Another person who the judges decide have the highest quality and most interesting posts will get the other gift card.
    
- How do you participate?
    
    Fill out this form [Insert form here] with your chapman email and LinkedIn URL and you will be automatically considered. Then every week fill out this form [Insert link here] linking to your post so we know you are still active
    
- Who is participating?
    
    We currently have 9 different clubs participating across many different colleges on campus! Use this as an opportunity to connect with them!
    
- How can I stay updated?
    - Follow us on instagram [@chapmanieee](https://www.instagram.com/chapmanieee/) ! This is the official source for all information about the challenge, rules, prizes, and more.
- I have another question
    
    Contact Joshua Dowd at [jdowd@chapman.edu](mailto:jdowd@chapman.edu) 
    

## Club Collaboration

Thanks for wanting to collaborate with us! IEEE is excited to get all of campus involved and connect students who otherwise might’ve not met. As far as how you come in, this section covers everything you need to know.

### What IEEE does for you

We will feature you guys in our promotional graphics, show meeting times and locations at the start of meetings, and highlight other things you ask. For us to do this, please send photos of your logos, meeting times and locations, and a 1-2 sentence summary of what your club does to [jdowd@chapman.edu](mailto:jdowd@chapman.edu). That way we can have the most accurate information given out to our members.
